Privacy policy

MTS (Mahjong Tournament Suite) is an app and website to run and follow Mahjong tournaments: tables, hands, rankings, statistics and the round timer. This policy covers the Android app MTS (com.etologic.mahjongtournamentsuite) and the website mts.mahjongmadrid.com.

Use without an account

Anyone can follow public tournaments without an account. Then the app only processes the anonymous data described under "Analytics" and "Error reports", and only if you accept it.

Accounts

There is no public sign-up. Tournament organizers create accounts for players, editors and administrators. An account holds:

  • Email address, used to sign in and to send the email to set or reset your password.
  • Alias (the name shown to others) and, if you or an organizer add one, a profile photo.
  • EMA (European Mahjong Association) player number, which links the account to your tournament results.
  • Role (player, editor, administrator).

Tournament data (player names, EMA numbers, countries, tables, scores and rankings) is entered by the organizers and is shown publicly as part of the tournament results, as is usual in EMA tournaments.

Analytics

On first start the app asks for your consent to anonymous analytics. If you decline, nothing is sent. If you accept, the app sends usage events to Google Analytics for Firebase (for example which screen was opened, how long it took to load, or that a hand was recorded). Events never contain your email, name, EMA number or any tournament id. You can withdraw consent at any time in the profile dialog. Google keeps this data for 14 months.

Error reports

If the app fails, it sends a technical error report (error type, stack trace, app version, platform). The server removes emails, long numbers and tokens from the text before saving it. Reports are deleted after 30 days.

Support form

If you write to us with the support form (mts.mahjongmadrid.com/support), we receive the name, email and message you enter, plus the app version and platform, only to answer you.

Security

The app uses Google Firebase (Authentication, Firestore, Cloud Storage, Cloud Functions, App Check and Analytics). Data is sent encrypted (HTTPS) and stored on Google servers; the database and the server are in the European Union. App Check verifies that requests come from the real app. We do not sell data and we do not show ads.

Who receives the data

Only Google, as the provider of Firebase, processes the data on our behalf. See Firebase privacy.

Your rights and deleting your account

You can change your alias and photo in your profile. To get a copy of your data, correct it, or delete your account and its data, send a request with the support form at mts.mahjongmadrid.com/support (also "Support" on the sign-in screen of the app) from the email of your account. We handle the request as soon as possible and delete the account, its email, alias and photo. Tournament results that are part of the official EMA records may be kept without your account. You can also complain to your data protection authority (in Spain, the AEPD).

Children

The app is not directed to children under 13.

Changes

We will publish any change on this page with a new date.

Comptes

Il n'y a pas d'inscription ouverte. Les organisateurs de tournois créent les comptes des joueurs, éditeurs et administrateurs. Un compte contient :

  • L'adresse e-mail, pour se connecter et pour recevoir l'e-mail de création ou de réinitialisation du mot de passe.
  • Le pseudo (le nom que voient les autres) et, si vous ou un organisateur en ajoutez une, une photo de profil.

Si vous acceptez, l'application envoie des événements d'utilisation à Google Analytics pour Firebase (par exemple l'écran ouvert, son temps de chargement ou l'enregistrement d'une main). Les événements ne contiennent jamais votre e-mail, votre nom, votre numéro EMA ni aucun identifiant de tournoi. Vous pouvez retirer votre accord à tout moment dans la fenêtre du profil. Google conserve ces données 14 mois.

Rapports d'erreur

Sécurité

L'application utilise Google Firebase (Authentication, Firestore, Cloud Storage, Cloud Functions, App Check et Analytics). Les données sont transmises chiffrées (HTTPS) et stockées sur des serveurs de Google ; la base de données et le serveur se trouvent dans l'Union européenne. App Check vérifie que les requêtes viennent de la vraie application. Nous ne vendons pas de données et n'affichons pas de publicité.

Destinataires des données

Seul Google, en tant que fournisseur de Firebase, traite les données pour notre compte. Voir la confidentialité de Firebase.

Vos droits et la suppression de votre compte

Vous pouvez modifier votre pseudo et votre photo dans votre profil. Pour obtenir une copie de vos données, les corriger, ou supprimer votre compte et ses données, envoyez la demande avec le formulaire d'assistance sur mts.mahjongmadrid.com/support (aussi « Assistance » sur l'écran de connexion de l'application) depuis l'e-mail de votre compte.
