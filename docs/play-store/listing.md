```
Follow Mahjong tournaments live: tables, hands, rankings and statistics.
```

**Full description**
```
MTS (Mahjong Tournament Suite) is the app to follow, play and run Mahjong tournaments with MCR rules.

FOLLOW ANY TOURNAMENT, NO ACCOUNT NEEDED
• Tournaments in play, upcoming and past, with filters by country and year.
• Tables of every round, with each hand as it is played.
• Live rankings of players and teams, chicken hands and best hand.
• The ranking after any round, ready to share as an image.
• Schedule, venue and directions, organizers and contact.

• Register from the app, with places and a public waiting list.
• Create your team and add your teammates by their EMA number.

ON TOURNAMENT DAY

• Scan the tournament QR code and your table opens.
• Links to the MTS website open straight in the app.
• Set the seats and enter the hands and penalties of your table from your phone.
• See every hand appear live, with the time left in the round.

YOUR RESULTS AND STATISTICS
• My results: every round of a tournament in one table, ready to share as an image.
• A chart of your place after each round.
• Skills, places, records and your toughest rivals.

RULES AT HAND
• The 81 MCR combinations with tile pictures.

FOR ORGANIZERS
• Registrations with waiting list, Google Form import and the draw of the pairings.
• Round clock that opens and closes the tables by itself.
• Big-screen ranking, table QR cards, ID cards and the EMA results report.

Available in English, Spanish, French, Italian, German, Swedish, Dutch and Russian, with light and dark themes. No ads.
```
