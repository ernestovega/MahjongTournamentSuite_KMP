2. PURPOSE AND AUDIENCE
- MTS (Mahjong Tournament Suite) is a free tool for Riichi and MCR Mahjong tournaments of the European Mahjong Association (EMA) and its national associations.
- Audience: tournament players, spectators and organizers.
- Problem it solves: tournaments were run on paper and spreadsheets.
- Value:
  • Automatic seating draw.
  • Players enter the hands of their table from their phone.
  • Live tables, rankings and statistics that anyone can follow.

3. HOW TO USE IT
- Without an account:
  • The app opens on the tournament list.
  • Tap a tournament to see its tables, hands, ranking, statistics and venue.
- Demo account (credentials in Sign-in Information):
  • An administrator.
  • Tap the Profile button (top right) and sign in.
  • The ADMIN button of the top bar opens the management area (tournaments, registrations, tables, rounds).
- Account deletion:
  • Please do not delete the demo account: it is not the last administrator, so it would really be deleted and the next review would have no demo account.
  • Option A, a disposable account ready to delete: {DELETE_ACCOUNT_EMAIL} / {DELETE_ACCOUNT_PASSWORD}
    Sign in with it, then Profile > Delete my account > Delete.
  • Option B, your own account: with the demo account open ADMIN > Users, tap +, enter an email you can read and tap "Save and send password reset".
    Set the password from the email, sign in with that account, then Profile > Delete my account > Delete.
